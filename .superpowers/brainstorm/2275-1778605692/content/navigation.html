Navigation pattern

Currently you have a hamburger menu top-left. On a tall S23 Ultra, the top corner is a stretch for thumbs. How should mobile nav work?

A

Keep hamburger (top-left)

No change to nav. All real-estate goes to content. Simple, but reaches are awkward on a 6.8" screen.

B

Bottom tab bar (4-5 primary destinations)

Native-feeling. Thumb-reachable. Eats ~56px of bottom space. Best for the most-used pages (Dashboard, Matchups, Decks, Cards). Less-used items live in a "More" tab or hamburger.

C

Collapsing top bar + hamburger

Header shrinks/hides on scroll-down, reappears on scroll-up. Reclaims vertical space when scrolling. Nav still via hamburger.

D

Bottom tab bar + collapsing top bar

Combines B and C. Maximum thumb-friendliness, maximum vertical reclaim. Most "native app" feel.
