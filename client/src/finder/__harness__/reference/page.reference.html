    Deck list

    How it decides

    The solver plays your Main Phase 1 from the exact card texts of your 57 cards and scores the end position: interruptions for their turn going first, damage and removal going second. With interruptions set, it also looks for lines that bait them out, then replays every candidate with each interruption used on your worst step and re-solves what you do next. Talisman, Ilia Silvia, Dragoon and Baronne answer their interruptions where the card text allows.

    Fuwalos, Purulia and Maxx "C": once one is live, every summon it counts gives them a card, and a card for them is weighed at a bit over half an interruption when the suggested line is picked. The other lines show the best board for more or fewer draws, so you can choose a different trade. Chained to one of your activations it counts that step; in answer to a summon that starts no chain it counts from the next one. For the Mulcharmy cards the End Phase hand limit is applied, assuming they hold 4 cards after discarding it.

    Against one interruption the worst case is exact: every step it could hit is tried. Against two or more it is close but can be slightly optimistic, because they are spent one after another, each where it hurts most. What a card on their field can do is a best guess from its text when you pick it by name: check the "on your turn" setting.

    It assumes: Ash stops adds, summons and sends from the Deck but not Sets; Super Polymerization can't be responded to; Solemn-type cards only stop summons that aren't made by a card effect; Circle's banish works when Pharaoh's Servant, DM of Black Magic or DM of Destruction is summoned. It doesn't model what their cards do when destroyed, or their own turn.
