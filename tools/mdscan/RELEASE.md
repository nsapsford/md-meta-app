**MD Board Scanner** reads your opponent's Master Duel field on PC and copies it for the DM Azamina Line Finder. It runs entirely on your PC: no internet, nothing sent anywhere.

Download `mdscan.exe` below, put it in its own folder, and run it. Windows may warn that it's from an unknown publisher (it isn't signed): choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| F7 | Set up for your screen (once, about 20 seconds) |
| F8 | Scan their whole field and copy it |
| F9 | Add the card shown in Master Duel's card detail panel |
| F10 | Clear the cards added with F9 |

Then press Ctrl+V anywhere on the line finder page.
